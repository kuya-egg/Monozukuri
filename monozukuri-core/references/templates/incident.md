# Incident writeup template

Copy the block below to `.monozukuri/incidents/YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` and fill it
in.

```markdown
# Incident: <title>

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Severity: <sev level>
- Status: mitigated | resolved

## Summary
<what happened, in two or three sentences>

## Impact
<who and what was affected, for how long, and how badly>

## Timeline (UTC)
- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — <event>
- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — <event>

## Detection
<how the incident was noticed: alert, report, or manual observation>

## Mitigation
<the actions taken to stop the bleeding and restore service>

## Root cause
<the underlying technical cause, traced to evidence>

## Contributing factors
<conditions that made the incident possible, likely, or worse>

## Prevention follow-ups
- <action> — owner: <name> — <target date>
- <action> — owner: <name> — <target date>

## Lessons
<what to carry forward: what worked, what to change>
```
