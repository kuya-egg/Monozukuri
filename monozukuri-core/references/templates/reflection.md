# Reflection template

Copy the block below to `.monozukuri/reflections/YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` and fill
it in. This is a hansei: honest review, not self-justification.

```markdown
# Reflection: <title>

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## What was the goal
<the outcome the work set out to achieve>

## What worked
<decisions, tools, or steps that paid off>

## What failed
<what went wrong or fell short, stated plainly>

## What caused rework
<the specific moments work had to be redone, and why>

## What assumptions were wrong
<beliefs held going in that reality contradicted>

## What could be automated
<toil or checks a machine should have done>

## One durable guardrail to propose
<a single concrete rule, test, or check to prevent a repeat>
```
